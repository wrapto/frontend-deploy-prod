# Wrapto Fees

Wrapto fees are composed of two parts:
**Network Fee** (blockchain transaction costs) and **Service Fee** (Wrapto platform fee).
The total fee you pay depends on whether you are *wrapping* or *unwrapping*.

---

## Wrapping Fee

When wrapping PAC into WPAC, the following fees apply:

* **Service Fee:**

  Service fee is 0.5% of the wrapped amount and minimum is 1 PAC and maximum is 40 PAC.

* **Network Fee:**

  The network fee depends on the destination network and is calculated based on the fee required by that network.
  This fee is then converted into PAC and deducted from the PAC amount you send in the bridge transaction.

---

## Unwrapping Fees

Unwrapping WPAC back to PAC is **completely free** for users.

The service fee is zero,
and the network fee required by the Pactus network to send PAC back to your wallet is **fully discounted by Wrapto**.
